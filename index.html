Keep useful records together.

A native SwiftUI app for compatible NFC tags, with Polish and English interfaces.

  • Read on iPhone.

    Inspect available NDEF records, tag technology, capacity and write access on a compatible physical iPhone.

  • Build your library.

    Save records, rename entries and organize favorites. The app uses SwiftData with private iCloud synchronization when configured and available.

  • Create and write.

    Create links, text and vCard contacts. Confirm before writing to a compatible writable tag; TapVault reads it back to verify the result.

From a tap to your next task.

  1. Read a tag.

    Start a scan on iPhone and hold a compatible tag near the NFC reader.

  2. Save the records.

    Choose a name and keep the content in your library, or create a new link, text or contact record.

  3. Write when ready.

    Check the destination, confirm the overwrite and keep the tag still until verification finishes.

Know what your tag supports.

Requires iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. iPad supports library and creator features; physical NFC reading and writing require a compatible iPhone. The simulator cannot perform NFC operations.

Supports compatible NDEF content on supported ISO 14443 / MIFARE, ISO 15693 and configured ISO 7816 NDEF tags. Proprietary applications, MIFARE Classic, card emulation and access-card cloning are not supported.

Scanning a tag does not create an Apple Wallet key. Wallet provisioning requires an issuer integration and is not implemented in this version.
